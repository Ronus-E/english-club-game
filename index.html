    Field manual

    How to play

    Move

    WASD or arrow keys. On touch, drag the joystick.

    Interact

    E or Enter, or the round ACTION button. Stand on a glowing station pad and interact.

    Pause

    Esc opens the pause menu and stops the timer.

    The 3-minute route

    1. 00:00 Welcome & guide
    2. 00:20 Vocabulary Garden
    3. 01:00 Grammar Classroom
    4. 01:40 Listening Lounge & Speaking Stage
    5. 02:20 American Culture Zone
    6. 02:50 Final Challenge Arena → results

    The timer is a challenge, not a punishment: every correct answer is banked immediately, and unfinished stations still appear in your skill report.

    Audio tip: listening tasks use your device's English voice. If no voice is available, the transcript appears automatically — the exercise remains fully solvable.

    About this project

    Mission: English Club

    An original educational game built for the American Corner English Clubs Exhibition. All pixel art is generated in code, all audio is synthesized live, and the whole game is cached locally after the first visit.

    • CEFR content: A1, A2 and B1 exercises across six skills plus American culture.
    • Accessibility: keyboard and touch controls, transcripts, text alternative for speaking, reduced motion, high contrast, screen-reader labels.
    • Privacy: no accounts, no trackers, no external requests. Progress lives in localStorage on this device.
    • Factual care: culture items distinguish verified facts from simplified classroom explanations; sources are listed in the documentation.

    Version 3.0 · Made for learners, not for data collection.
