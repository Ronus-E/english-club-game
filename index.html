    Quick guide

    How the quiz works

    One quiz

    Choose A1, A2 or B1. You answer fifteen questions: three each in vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing and American culture.

    Answer

    Tap an option, type a short answer or put words in order. The correct answer and a short explanation appear immediately.

    Five skills

    1. Vocabulary — words and meanings
    2. Grammar — tenses and structures
    3. Reading — short texts and notices
    4. Writing — build or complete a sentence
    5. American culture — verified facts

    There is no clock. Your report shows a grade per skill and two suggestions for what to practise next.

    About this project

    English Club Quiz

    An offline educational quiz for the American Corner English Club. All artwork is generated in code, all sounds are synthesised live, and the whole quiz is cached locally after the first visit.

    • CEFR content: A1, A2 and B1 exercises across four skills plus American culture.
    • Accessibility: keyboard and touch support, reduced motion, high contrast, screen-reader labels.
    • Privacy: no accounts, no trackers, no external requests. Progress lives in localStorage on this device.
    • Factual care: culture items distinguish verified facts from simplified classroom explanations; sources are listed in the documentation.

    Version 4.0 · Made for learners, not for data collection.
